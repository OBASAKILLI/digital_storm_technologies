KRA eTIMS Compliance

Get eTIMS Ready — Without the Headache

The Kenya Revenue Authority's eTIMS mandate requires all VAT-registered businesses to submit tax invoices electronically. We make it simple — integrating directly into your existing systems.

1
System Audit & KRA Registration We assess your existing software and handle your eTIMS device registration with KRA on your behalf.
2
API Integration We connect your ERP, POS, or billing system directly to KRA's eTIMS API for real-time invoice submission.
3
Testing, Training & Go Live Full system testing, staff training, and 24/7 support for a smooth, penalty-free launch.
